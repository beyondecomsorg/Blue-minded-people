ABOUT US

Where Craftsmanship Meets Innovation At Blue Minded People, every garment begins its journey through our strategic manufacturing partnership at a state-of-the-art facility in Doddaballapur, Karnataka, India. Established in 2010, this fully integrated production ecosystem brings together advanced technology, skilled craftsmanship, and responsible manufacturing practices to create premium denim and apparel for global fashion markets. Built on a foundation of quality, precision, and innovation, the partner facility manages every stage of the production process under one roof. From fabric preparation and garment construction to washing, finishing, quality assurance, and warehousing, each step is carefully monitored to ensure exceptional consistency and product excellence.
